# MediAI Medical Center — Hospital Overview, Campuses, and Facilities

## About MediAI Medical Center
MediAI — AI Hospital Patient Experience & Operations Platform is a unified hospital network delivering outpatient consultations, inpatient hospital care, diagnostic laboratory services, pharmacy tracking, emergency trauma care, and digital patient portal access across four regional medical campuses.
