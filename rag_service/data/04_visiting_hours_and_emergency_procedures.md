# MediAI Visiting Hours, Visitor Policies, and Emergency Procedures

## Official Hospital Visiting Hours
MediAI Medical Center maintains structured visiting hours to balance family support with patient rest and infection control:

- **General Inpatient Wards (Wards 2A–5C):** 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM daily. Maximum of 2 visitors per bedside at a time.
- **Intensive Care Units (ICU & CICU):** 12:00 PM – 2:00 PM and 5:00 PM – 7:00 PM daily. Restricted to immediate family members only, with a maximum of 2 visitors per session.
- **Pediatric Care Pavilion:** Parents and primary legal guardians have **24/7 bedside access**.
- **Emergency Department (Main Campus):** Open **24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year** for emergency medical care.

## Visitor Check-In and Safety Guidelines
- All visitors must sign in with guest reception upon entering inpatient areas.
- Visitors must sanitize their hands at nurse checkpoints before entering patient rooms or intensive care units.
- Please adhere to bedside visitor limits (2 visitors per bedside in General Inpatient Wards and ICU/CICU).

## Emergency Care and Acute Symptom Protocol
- If you or someone nearby experiences life-threatening symptoms—such as acute or crushing chest pain, severe shortness of breath or difficulty breathing, signs of stroke (such as sudden numbness or weakness), severe trauma, or active uncontrolled bleeding—**call 911 immediately** or proceed directly to the nearest Emergency Room.
- **MediAI Main Campus Emergency Department:** Open 24/7/365 at 400 Medical Center Way, Suite 100, Metro Medical District.
- **Direct 24/7 Emergency Line:** +1 (555) 234-8099.
- Emergency triage uses the five-level Emergency Severity Index (ESI Levels 1–5) to ensure critical patients receive immediate intervention.

## Non-Diagnosis Medical Safety Policy
The MediAI AI Hospital Assistant provides general hospital operational information and portal navigation only. It does **not** diagnose medical conditions, prescribe medications, recommend specific dosages, or replace professional clinical evaluation by a licensed physician. Always consult your attending physician for medical advice.
