# MediAI Clinical Specialties, Medical Services, and Diagnostics

## Accredited Clinical Specialties
MediAI Medical Center operates 10 accredited clinical specialties staffed by board-certified attending physicians across its four regional campuses:
1. **Cardiology & Vascular Medicine** — Cardiovascular evaluations, ECG monitoring, hypertension management, and specialized vascular care.
2. **Neurology & Neurosciences** — Neurological assessments, stroke follow-up, and specialized neurological care.
3. **General Internal Medicine** — Primary adult care, preventive screenings, chronic disease management, and annual comprehensive metabolic reviews.
4. **Emergency Medicine & Trauma** — 24/7/365 acute care and emergency stabilization at the Main Campus Emergency Department.
5. **Pediatrics & Neonatology** — Pediatric outpatient visits, neonatal care, and inpatient care at the Pediatric Care Pavilion.
6. **Oncology & Hematology** — Comprehensive cancer care and hematological consultations.
7. **Orthopedic Surgery** — Musculoskeletal evaluations, orthopedic surgical planning, and rehabilitation follow-up.
8. **Obstetrics & Gynecology** — Women's health, prenatal care, obstetrics, and gynecological services.
9. **Radiology & Diagnostic Imaging** — Outpatient and inpatient diagnostic imaging services.
10. **Pulmonology & Critical Care** — Respiratory medicine, pulmonary evaluations, and intensive care support.

## Diagnostic Laboratory & Pathology Services
MediAI provides integrated laboratory diagnostics synchronized directly with the Patient Portal. Standard diagnostic panels available through physician orders include:
- Comprehensive Metabolic Panel (CMP)
- Complete Blood Count (CBC)
- Lipid Profile Panels
- Cardiac biomarkers such as Troponin-I
- Endocrine panels such as HbA1c

Finalized lab reports include numeric result values, clinical reference ranges, status flags (Normal, Abnormal, or Critical), and physician review sign-off. Patients can view finalized diagnostic reports inside the **Lab Results** tab of the Patient Portal.

## Pharmacy & Prescription Services
During clinical consultations, attending physicians issue digital e-prescriptions linked to the patient's encounter record. Prescription details include medication name, dosage, frequency, route of administration, duration, refill eligibility, and clinician instructions. Patients can track both active and historical prescriptions inside the **Prescriptions** tab of the Patient Portal.
